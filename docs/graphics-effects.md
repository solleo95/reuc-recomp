# Graphics effects

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-graphics-enhancements-design.md`; plan of phases 0–3: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-graphics-enhancements-p0-3.md`; survey of the game's lighting: `docs/spikes/S6-graphics-survey.md`.

All effects live in the renderer (GPU passes recorded on the render thread); the guest threads are never touched and no effect reads pixels back. Every one is a separate option in the launcher (Graphics page) and in the in-game menu (Insert, Effects tab), saved in `config.json` and overridable on the command line. All off with anisotropy 1 is the original picture.

| SMAA 1x | `upscaler` | `--upscaler smaa` | a value of Anti-aliasing / upscaling | the effects path |
| Per-pixel lighting | `per_pixel_lighting` | `--per-pixel-lighting` | on/off (off) | the effects path |
| Ambient occlusion (GTAO) | `ambient_occlusion` | `--ambient-occlusion` | off, low, medium, high, ultra (off) | the effects path |

"The effects path" = `VK_EXT_dynamic_rendering_unused_attachments` + `independentBlend` (the same the upscalers need); without it those options are greyed out with a reason (`uc.exe --features` reports `effects`, `effects_reason`, `anisotropy_max`).

## How it renders

* The 3D scene of a frame renders into the internal scene targets (colour RGBA8, depth D32, motion RG16F, G-buffer RGBA16) up to the scene boundary (`gx::SceneBoundary`). Without an upscaler the targets exist only while an effect needs them (a blit pass-through at ratio 1); switching that on or off rebuilds the targets, every other toggle is live.
* G-buffer (`renderer/gbuffer.h`): octahedral view normal, GX fog factor, flags; written by depth-writing draws only.
* At the boundary, before the upscaler: `renderer/occlusion_pass.cpp` runs GTAO (horizon search at half or full resolution, depth-aware blur) and the contact-shadow march, then multiplies both into the scene colour, faded by the fog factor.
* Per-pixel lighting is a push-constant bit in the motion shader variants: the GX lighting equations run on the interpolated view normal and position instead of per vertex.
* SMAA is an `Upscaler` at ratio 1 (compute, `third_party/smaa`); no jitter, no vectors.
* Presets (budget per effect on the owner's RTX 4080, measured with the pass timestamps): GTAO medium + contact shadows 0.30 ms and high 0.50 ms at the harness size (limits 0.4 / 1.0 ms).

## Bloom

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-bloom-design.md`; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-bloom.md`. The renderer's own bloom (`renderer/bloom_pass.{h,cpp}`, `shaders/bloom.slang`, math in `renderer/bloom_math.h`) replaces the game's: the game's composite draw (`gx::bloom_composite`) is always skipped, off means no bloom at all (`graphics.bloom=false` or strength 0).

* Runs at the scene boundary after the upscaler, motion blur and the DLSS 5 hook, before the HUD, on the output-size EFB colour. A six-level RGBA16F pyramid (level 0 is half size): the first level reads the 8-bit colour in linear light (gamma 2.2), expands it above a knee (`bloom_expand`: 1.0 counts as 4x white in SDR, peak/paper white in HDR; the buffer is 8-bit, so this is an estimate), keeps only the energy above a soft threshold (`bloom_soft_threshold`: 0.6, knee 0.3; without it every lit wall turned the picture milky) and averages 4x4 texels with Karis weights; the next levels are 13-tap downsamples; the glow is the weighted sum (0.20 0.20 0.18 0.16 0.14 0.12) of tent upsamples, scaled by the mix (`bloom_mix`: 0.15 x strength in SDR, x2.5 more in HDR). No temporal state: deterministic runs stay identical.
* SDR: the glow is added to the EFB in place (gamma 2.2, a static ordered dither of +-0.5 LSB), so the HUD drawn afterwards is untouched. HDR: the glow stays in a float image (`BloomPass::glow_view`) that `shaders/hdr.slang` adds in nits at the HDR encode (the present and frame generation's HUD-less copy), so it passes paper white up to the peak; the encode adds it over the HUD too (owner option A; option B, separate scene and HUD layers, is the fallback if that looks bad). Captures stay SDR, so they show no glow in HDR.
* Constants are starting values tuned by eye; they live in `bloom_math.h` and `bloom.slang` (mirrored, tested in `test_bloom_math`). Frame log and `--profile`: `GpuPass::bloom`.
* Tests: `test_bloom_math`, `renderer_effects_smoke --bloom` (falloff, symmetry, strength, determinism, menu frames, render scale; the HDR part runs only when Windows HDR is on for the window).

## Ray tracing

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-ray-tracing-design.md`; plans `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-ray-tracing-p0-1.md` (device support, acceleration structures, material records, denoiser) and `…-p2.md` (shadows); measurements `docs/spikes/S8-ray-tracing.md`. Only the shadows work so far; reflections, lighting and ambient occlusion with ray tracing follow (their options exist in `config.json` and on the command line but have no effect and no UI).

* The master switch turns the active ray-traced options on and off; the levels are kept. An option is active when the master is on and its level is not `off`. Unsupported GPUs (no ray query, fewer than 5 colour attachments) ignore all of them (`--features`: `ray_tracing_reason`).
* Each frame's scene draws become a view-space acceleration structure (one BLAS per packet, the TLAS at the scene boundary, `renderer/rt_scene.cpp`). While RT shadows are active the scene pass also writes a per-pixel material record index and the albedo (`renderer/rt_material.h`).
* `renderer/shaders/rt_shadow.slang`: per pixel the draw's own GX lights are evaluated with the GX lighting equations, one ray query per selected light, and the factor `1 − Σ share_i·(1 − visibility_i)` is written at 1/divisor of the internal resolution; the own denoiser (`renderer/rt_denoiser.cpp`) filters it and a depth-aware composite multiplies the scene colour on lit, model-valid pixels, faded by the GX fog. Directional lights follow `rt_shadows`, point and spot lights `rt_local_shadows` (the strongest 1/2/4/8). The structure has two occluder classes (instance masks): static draws (the level) and skinned draws (per-vertex matrices: characters, enemies). The game's lights come in three kinds: the car's lamps (point lights in the level: traced against the whole scene under `rt_local_shadows`), the characters' rig lights (weak directional fill lights that ignore the level: traced against skinned draws only, under `rt_shadows`; tracing them against the level turned every character black indoors) and the specular lights of channel 1 (never traced; the specular channel is accepted by the colour model and dims with the diffuse part). Only lit, model-valid draws receive a shadow (about 90% of the fight demo's records). Alpha cut-outs cast no ray-traced shadow yet. A lamp's ray ends `kRtLampClearance` (1 view unit) short of the lamp, so a lamp inside its own shade or chandelier does not black out the surfaces around it; the shadows of the fixture arms on the ceiling remain. The game's point lights are camera-relative (in the first scene's first seconds every lamp keeps its view-space z: -42.7, +70.7, -72.1, +177.7, while the chandelier model comes closer; the seats are lit by one at the eye, view position about (0.1, 2.8, 0.1)), so shadows cast toward them swim with the camera and tracing them was dropped (`VulkanRenderer::set_rt_trace_lamps` turns it back on for tests). Under `rt_local_shadows` a synthetic key light casts the level's shadows instead (`rt_shadow_math.h`): a direction fixed in the world, well off the camera's axis (to the right, above and a little toward the camera: a light near the axis sees what the camera sees and casts no visible shadow; its view-space direction starts at (0.62, 0.72, 0.31) and is turned every frame by the camera's rotation, `MotionTracker::view_to_prev_view` and `rt_key_direction_after`, back to the default on a camera cut), so neither a camera translation nor a rotation moves its shadows; one soft ray per pixel, blocked only within `kRtOverheadReach` = 8 units so the car's roof does not shadow everything; surfaces that face it lose `kRtOverheadStrength` = 65% of the lit colour where blocked (the game's own shading is baked into the colour). "RT shadows" therefore shadows characters (rig lights, from characters and enemies) and "RT local shadows" shadows the level (lamps, from everything).
* Static draws of a mesh the renderer has uploaded (the level) do not rebuild their geometry every frame: the mesh's object-space BLAS is built once (`renderer/rt_mesh_cache.h` policy: 256 MiB budget, at most 16 builds per frame, least recently used leaves first, a freed mesh drops its BLAS once no frame in flight can use it) and each draw adds a TLAS instance with its object-to-view matrix. A mesh whose BLAS is still being built does not occlude for that frame. Skinned draws keep the per-frame view-space BLAS.
* Presets (`renderer/rt_shadow_math.h`): the resolution divisor, rays per pixel and soft edges per level; tuned against the budget (High ≈ 100 FPS for everything ray-traced on the RTX 4080).
* Ray-traced ambient occlusion (`rt_ambient_occlusion`, `renderer/rt_ao_math.h`): cosine-weighted hemisphere rays of a limited length (radius 1.5 to 5 view units: a seat is about 4 tall), the share that hits something lowers the colour by 70 to 80%; it rides in the same visibility pass and signal as the shadows (one denoiser, one composite, the finest divisor of the active effects) and applies to every GX-lit surface with a normal, not only light-model-valid ones (unlit surfaces are baked or emissive and stay untouched; with shadows alone the composite still gates on the light model). It replaces GTAO while it is on (the launcher and the menu grey the GTAO combo). Cost and the first-scene pairs: `docs/spikes/S11-rt-ao.md`.
* Ray-traced reflections (`rt_reflections`, `renderer/rt_reflection_pass.cpp`, `rt_reflect_math.h`): mirror rays on the surfaces the game marks glossy (the specular material colour of channel 1: reflectivity in bits 12 to 19 of the record flags); the hit's colour is the frame's own colour at the hit's screen position when the hit is on screen and not hidden, so only what the camera sees is reflected; own radiance denoiser; composite by fresnel x reflectivity x 4. Subtle in the dark first scene (S12).
* Ray-traced lighting (`rt_lighting`, `renderer/rt_light_math.h`): one diffuse bounce as a second mode of the reflection pass: cosine-weighted rays, the hit's colour from the frame (only what the camera sees), albedo x mean hit colour x strength added to lit surfaces; it only adds light and invents none. Mild in the dark first scene (S13).
* DLSS Ray Reconstruction (`ray_reconstruction`, `renderer/rr_inputs_pass.cpp`, `rr_math.h`): on DLSS frames with an RT effect the effects composite their undenoised signals and one RR evaluation denoises and upscales the picture instead of the own denoiser plus Super Resolution (guides: the RT albedo target, normal + roughness, specular albedo). Cleaner, about 1.3 to 1.5 ms dearer, and the glow sprites around lights disappear (S14).
* While RT shadows are active the shadow map and contact shadows do not run; the launcher and the in-game menu grey their options ("Заменено трассировкой лучей") and the saved values come back with the master switch off.
* Timing: frame log `gpu_rt_as_ms`, `gpu_rt_shadows_ms`; `--profile` prints `rt_shadows`.

* GPU: `renderer_effects_smoke [--validation] [--aniso] [--smaa] [--gbuffer] [--passthrough] [--gtao] [--contact] [--ppl] [--rt-material] [--rt-as] [--rt-shadows] [--rt-cache] [--rt-reflections] [--rt-lighting] [--rt-rr] [--rt-denoise]` (every section when no flag), `renderer_upscale_smoke`, `renderer_smoke`. They are not part of ctest; run them from `build/renderer` (with `VK_LOADER_LAYERS_DISABLE=~implicit~` for `--validation`). `UC_SMOKE_DUMP=<dir>` saves the frames of the sections.
* Live: `python -m uctest run fight --game-arg=--ambient-occlusion --game-arg=high --game-arg=--profile` prints `[gpu] passes: …` per second; the frame log has `gpu_upscale_ms`, `gpu_smaa_ms`, `gpu_occlusion_ms`.
* Debug switches: `UC_LIGHT_STATS=<file>` (lighting per callsite), `UC_SKIP_CALLSITES=<hex>,…` (leave draws of a guest callsite out).
